    7questions

    How do I start working with your office?expand_more

    Fill in the quote wizard or call; the document list and a draft engagement letter are shared at the initial meeting.

    Is it hard to change accountants?expand_more

    A handover needs prior ledgers and returns; we handle the handover correspondence together.

    How do I send documents?expand_more

    As photos, PDFs or via the e-invoice portal; sending them by the 10th of each month is recommended.

    What if an invoice is lost?expand_more

    A copy is requested from the issuer; for e-invoices the portal record is enough.

    When is the VAT return filed?expand_more

    As a general rule, by the 28th of the following month. Dates are for information only; follow Revenue Administration announcements for the official tax calendar and extensions.

    When is the annual income tax return?expand_more

    As a general rule, during March. Dates are for information only; follow Revenue Administration announcements for the official tax calendar and extensions.

    Why are your fees not on the site?expand_more

    Fees are set in writing at the meeting according to business type and workload; under professional rules, prices are not advertised on the site.
